A mathematical epoch length. A length of ROOT_DISTANCE blocks or fewer still assigns heights to epochs, but holds no root and therefore no transition.

A position within a mathematical epoch. Genesis has offset zero in epoch one.
EpochSchedule
The activation epoch is explicit: mathematical epoch assignment alone never enables epochs. The length always holds a root, so every dynamic epoch has a source.

A nonempty inclusive window of positive epochs, independent of a particular retention policy.
