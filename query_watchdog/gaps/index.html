Functions§

find_gaps
Gaps of heights, whose range HEAD answered 404: first_missing, then gap_end, repeated after each gap from a HEAD of the rest. Gaps are marked NotFound; after a failure the rest is marked Failed(failure); every mark lies inside heights. Known::Gap keeps HEAD’s two-end check.
first_missing 🔒
First missing height of heights, given that their HEAD answered 404: binary search over prefixes [heights.start, mid), at most ceil(log2(len)) HEADs (7 for 100 heights). lo is the end of a prefix known present, hi the end of a prefix known to hold a 404.
gap_end 🔒
End of the gap that starts at the missing height first, below end: gallop, then binary search over single heights.
gap_still_missing 🔒
Whether the first and the last height of a known gap are both still missing, which keeps the whole row; a present first height is checked first, so a refilled front costs one request.
next_gap 🔒
The first gap in rest, None when every height is present. A HEAD of rest comes first unless it is already known to be 404.
single 🔒
HEAD of one height.
